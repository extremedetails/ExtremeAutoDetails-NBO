# Extreme Auto Details - Nairobi

> We bring extreme premium automobile care directly to your doorstep. From systematic exterior cleaning and wheel restoration to thorough interior deep cleaning and protective finishes, we maintain your car's ultimate value.

---

## 🚘 About The Project

**Extreme Auto Details** provides high-end, mobile detailing services across Nairobi. This repository contains the codebase for our official website, allowing clients to explore service packages, learn about our detailing processes, and request doorstep appointments.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Mobile Doorstep Booking:** Request professional auto detailing delivered directly to your home or office.
* **Comprehensive Service Packages:** Browse through exterior paint protection, interior deep cleans, and ceramic coating options.
* **Wheel & Paint Restoration:** Tailored treatments to eliminate contaminants and restore your vehicle's factory shine.
* **Mobile-Responsive Design:** Quick and easy booking experience across all mobile devices and desktops.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla ES6+)
* **Hosting:** GitHub Pages / Vercel
